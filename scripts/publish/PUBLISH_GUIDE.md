# How to publish the article (zero cost, ~20 minutes)

This gets real dofollow backlinks to openapihub.410185103.workers.dev - the
single most effective zero-cost action for getting the site out of Google's
"new site" sandbox and into actual search results.

The article is already written for you at:
```
scripts/publish/best-free-apis-2026.md
```
(Also downloadable from your live site at /best-free-apis-2026.md)

## Step 1 - dev.to (highest value, do this first)

dev.to gives dofollow links and has a huge developer audience. A decent
listicle here regularly gets 2,000-10,000 reads.

1. Go to https://dev.to and sign up (free, GitHub login works).
2. Click **Create Post** (top right).
3. Click the gear icon next to the title field, paste in:
   - Tags: `webdev, api, beginners, programming`
4. Open `scripts/publish/best-free-apis-2026.md` in any text editor
   (Notepad is fine), select all, copy.
5. Paste into the dev.to editor. The front matter (the `---` block at the top)
   will be parsed automatically into the title, tags, and canonical URL.
6. Click **Publish**.

That is it. dev.to handles the rest.

## Step 2 - Hashnode (also dofollow, also free)

1. Go to https://hashnode.com and sign up (free).
2. Click **Write** (top bar) -> **New Post**.
3. Paste the same Markdown. Hashnode also parses the front matter.
4. Set the publication to your personal blog (free, created automatically).
5. Publish.

## Step 3 - Reddit (no backlink value for SEO, but real traffic)

Post to r/webdev or r/programming. Do NOT just drop a link - Reddit downvotes
that. Instead post the article body as a text post, with a single link at the
end: "Full directory with 1581 more: openapihub.410185103.workers.dev".

## Step 4 - Hacker News (high variance, occasionally huge)

Submit https://news.ycombinator.com/submit -> link to your live best-of page
(https://openapihub.410185103.workers.dev/best-free-apis-2026), not the
article. Title something honest like "Show HN: I catalogued 1581 free public
APIs". Most submissions get little traction, but one good day = thousands of
visitors.

---

## What you are actually accomplishing

Each dev.to / Hashnode post creates a dofollow backlink to your site. Google
uses backlinks as the main signal for ranking a new site. Without any
backlinks, a brand-new site on a `.workers.dev` subdomain ranks for nothing
- it is effectively invisible. With 2-3 good backlinks from dev.to / Hashnode /
HN, the site starts appearing in search results within 2-6 weeks.

This is why this step matters more than any code change: it is the difference
between a site that exists and a site that gets found.

## What NOT to do

- Do not post the same article to dev.to AND Medium with no canonical. Pick
  ONE as the canonical (the article is already set to your site as canonical,
  so dev.to knows your site is the original).
- Do not buy backlinks or use "submit to 500 directories" services. Google
  penalises these and they can sink the site permanently.
- Do not spam. One good post per platform, then move on.
